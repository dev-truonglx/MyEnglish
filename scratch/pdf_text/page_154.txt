III. Trạng từ (adverbs)
Trạng từ thường đứng ở các vị trí sau:
1. Sau trợ động từ và trước động từ thường.
Ex: The South of Vietnam was completely liberated on April 30, 1975.
I have recently finished my homework.
2. Trước tính từ.
Ex: I’m truthfully grateful to you for your help.
Nitric oxide is highly harmful.
I feel completely interested in this book.
3. Sau too.
Ex: He studied too lazily to pass his exam.
She came too late to see him yesterday.
4. Trong cấu trúc so ...that.
Ex: Jack drove so fast that he caused an accident.
The child ate so much that he was sick.
