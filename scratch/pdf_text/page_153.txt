II. Tính từ (adjectives)
Tính từ thường đứng ở các vị trí sau:
1. Trước danh từ.
Ex: Modern society needs engineers.
Adj N
2. Sau hệ từ.
Ex: She is beautiful.
Tom seems tired now.
Peter felt upset because he failed the test.
Mary looks happy now.
Ex: We should make our school tidy.
O adj
3. Sau too.
Ex: He is too short to play basketball.
Daisy is too young to drive a car.
4. Trước enough.
Ex: She is tall enough to play volleyball.
Ben is rich enough to buy such an expensive house.
adj + N
be/ seem/ appear/ feel/ taste/ look/ keep + adj
keep/ make + O + adj
be/ seem/ look/... + too + adj
be + adj + enough
